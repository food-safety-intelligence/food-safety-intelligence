A note on this score

What it isn't.

This score is a model estimate, not a verdict. A "High" prediction does not mean this restaurant is unsafe to eat at today, nor that an inspection failure is imminent. It means the public record — past inspections, nearby complaints, license details — contains patterns that historically precede a failed inspection or priority violation in the following six months.

If you are immunocompromised, the top drivers panel above is usually more actionable than the score itself. Recurring temperature-related violations, for instance, are the kind of thing your care team would tell you to avoid.
