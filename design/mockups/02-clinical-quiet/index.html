For caregivers

If you're choosing for someone with compromised immunity, the drivers matter more than the score.

Two restaurants can share the same score for different reasons. We show the top three drivers — recurring temperature violations, nearby pest complaints, long gaps since last inspection — so you can match the data to the precautions your care team recommends.
